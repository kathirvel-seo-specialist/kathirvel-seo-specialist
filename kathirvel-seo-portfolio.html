Featured SEO case studies

Technical SEO · JavaScript

Marine job portal: fixing JavaScript indexing

1,000+ job URLs existed, but only about 10 were indexed. JavaScript displayed only the latest jobs, so deep URLs were invisible to crawlers.

What I did

  • Checked Search Console indexing states and site: searches
  • Audited structure and JavaScript behaviour with developers
  • Recommended scalable pagination and stronger internal linking
  • Optimized titles and descriptions, added JobPosting schema
  • 1,000+ URLs indexed
  • Page 1 rankings
  • ~40 leads / mo
  • 300+ ChatGPT citations

Large websites need an architecture that makes important URLs discoverable, crawlable, indexable, and connected.

Analytics · Investigation

The Singapore traffic spike

A single day showed 1,118 Singapore users against a baseline of about 40. Rather than reporting artificial growth, I validated it across systems.

Forensic Triangulation

  1. GA4
  2. Search Console
  3. Landing pages
  4. Device/browser
  5. Apache logs
  6. Cloudflare
  • GA4 showed direct traffic; Search Console showed no organic rise
  • Hundreds of URLs hit, with unusually low engagement (< 1s)
  • Server logs showed no matching legitimate requests
  • Cloudflare recorded 91 managed challenges, 22 blocked outright
  • Bot traffic confirmed
  • Reporting clean
  • Zero ranking drop

Investigation, evidence, and risk assessment are vital—never rely solely on one platform for truth.

Methodology

My SEO process

  1. Understand the businessModel, audience, services, locations, goals, and competitors.
  2. ResearchKeywords, intent, SERPs, content gaps, and market opportunities.
  3. AuditTechnical, content, on-page, links, indexing, local setup, and analytics.
  4. PrioritizeScored by Impact × Effort × Business value to avoid low-ROI busywork.
  5. ImplementFixes, content, schema, local SEO, and developer sprint coordination.
  6. Measure and improveRankings, clicks, leads, GBP, and AI visibility, then iterate.

How I investigate a traffic drop

When traffic or rankings shift, I follow a rigorous sequential diagnosis:

  1. Search Console
  2. GA4
  3. Crawl & indexing
  4. Server & rendering
  5. SERP changes
  6. Root cause
  7. Prioritized fix
  8. Re-measure

I separate correlation from the actual problem. My diagnostic reports answer: what changed, why, what the business impact is, and what to do next.

AI in my SEO work

I use AI for keyword clustering, intent analysis, content research and briefs, competitor analysis, schema assistance, data analysis, reporting, and workflow automation, then validate output with SEO tools and real-world search telemetry.

AI can accelerate the work. Human SEO judgment decides what should be done.
